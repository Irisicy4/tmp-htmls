Finally, visit Crunchbase (https://www.crunchbase.com/) and find each company's funding stage, total funding raised, and most recent funding round date. Compile a Head of Product opportunity report table with columns: Company, Job Title, Work Mode, Salary Range, Glassdoor Rating, CEO Approval, Funding Stage, Total Funding, Last Round Date, and a "priority tier" (High/Medium/Low) based on growth signals.
