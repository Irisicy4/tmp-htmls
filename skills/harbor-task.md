# Skill: Finding Budget-Friendly Alternatives for Products

## Task Pattern
This skill applies when the agent needs to identify and compare budget-friendly alternatives to a specific product, ensuring that the alternatives meet similar feature requirements.

## Strategy
1. Identify the target product and its key features by navigating to its webpage.
2. Extract relevant product information, including features and pricing.
3. Search for alternative products within the specified budget on the same or similar platforms.
4. Filter and summarize the alternatives based on their features and prices, comparing them to the original product.
5. Present the findings in a clear and concise format.

## Key Action Steps
- Navigate to the product's webpage to gather information.
- Extract key features and pricing details from the product description.
- Use search functionality to find similar products within the budget.
- Filter results based on relevant keywords (e.g., "backpack") to ensure relevance.
- Summarize the alternatives, highlighting key features and prices for comparison.

## Tips and Pitfalls
- Ensure to focus on the main features that define the product's utility; avoid getting sidetracked by less relevant details.
- Be mindful of the budget constraint; always check that alternatives are under the specified price limit.
- If the original product is unavailable, consider using similar products that may have slight variations in features but still meet the user's needs.
- Double-check the credibility of the sources and the availability of the alternatives before finalizing the recommendations.
